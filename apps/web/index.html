What we didn't build

CherryTor avoids insecure anti-patterns common in traditional scrapers to guarantee zero client exposure and zero open proxy vulnerabilities.

× No Generic Proxy

INV-01 & INV-02 strictly prohibit ?target= or arbitrary client-supplied URLs. Edge fetches only server-registered origins.

× No Raw HTML Relay

INV-04 & INV-05 ensure all responses are validated into structured JSON. Raw HTML feeds and script injections are dropped.

× No Storage Secrets

INV-08 & INV-07 prevent storing passwords or RPC credentials in browser storage, eliminating credential scraping risks.
